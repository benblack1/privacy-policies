Privacy Policy for MusikaJournal

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Summary: MusikaJournal is designed with privacy by default. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All your journal entries, photos, practice logs, and settings remain exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MusikaJournal does not require an account, registration, or login. The application does not request network access permissions and does not transmit data across the internet to any developer-owned or third-party servers.

2. On-Device Data Storage and Processing

  • Practice & Journal Data: All entries, notes, logs, and dates are stored in a local database stored solely within your device's protected internal storage.
  • Biometric Authentication: If you enable biometric lock (fingerprint or face unlock), authentication is managed strictly by the Android operating system. MusikaJournal never collects, receives, or stores your biometric credentials.
  • Text Recognition (OCR): Features utilizing text recognition (Google ML Kit) process images entirely on your device. Images and scanned text are never uploaded to the cloud or external servers.
  • Notifications: Practice reminders are scheduled and triggered locally by the device's WorkManager / Alarm system. No push notification servers are used.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

MusikaJournal does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics providers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or behavioral trackers.

4. Data Backup, Export, and Sharing

Because MusikaJournal does not operate cloud servers:

  • If you choose to export your journal data or share files using Android's system share sheet, that action is initiated entirely by you.
  • Device backups (such as Google Drive system backups) are managed according to your Android device settings and Google's privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

MusikaJournal does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction). It is completely safe for musicians of all ages.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published to this page with an updated effective date.
