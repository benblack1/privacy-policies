Privacy Policy for Brownie Points Counter

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Summary: Brownie Points Counter is built by Blackend Inc as a completely free, offline utility app. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal data. All your points and counter data remain exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Brownie Points Counter operates entirely on your device. We do not require an account, registration, or login. The application does not request network access permissions, operates without internet connectivity, and does not collect, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information or usage data.

2. On-Device Data Storage

All data generated by using the application is stored strictly on your local device:

  • Counter Data: Your brownie points count is stored locally within your device's protected internal storage (SharedPreferences). It is never sent to external servers or accessible to third parties.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Your counter data persists locally on your device until you reset it within the app or uninstall the application / clear app data through your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Brownie Points Counter does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics), or behavioral trackers.

The application is distributed via Google Play and may rely on baseline Google Play Services for standard platform functionality (such as app installation, updates, and core Android runtime services). For details on how Google processes platform data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

Brownie Points Counter does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). It is completely safe for users of all ages and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations regarding minors.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
